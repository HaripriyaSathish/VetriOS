Vetri OS – Roles
Vetri OS currently has 8 roles. Each person signs in with one or more roles, and the role decides which menus they see and what they can do. Use this list as the reference for who the redesigned flows are for.
#	Role	What this role is, in one line
1	System Administrator	Full control of VetriOS: manages roles, accounts and permissions, approves requests, and sees every module including audit logs.
2	HR Administrator	Runs HR day to day: employees, attendance, leave, worklogs, onboarding, promotions, payroll references and exit management.
3	Business Team	Handles student enquiries and enrollment coordination, internship approvals and audit-log oversight.
4	Project Manager	Leads teams and projects: creates and updates projects and training batches and views employee and report data.
5	Employee	Standard staff member who uses their own dashboard, worklogs, leave and the document library.
6	Intern	Trainee inside the company who sees only their own internship space and can view documents shared with them.
7	Student	Enrolled learner who sees only their own courses and progress, with no administrative permissions.
8	Viewer	Read-only role that can look at documents, employees, projects, training and reports but cannot change anything.
Note: Trainer is not a separate role. A Trainer is an Employee who has a Trainer Profile and is assigned to a training batch.

What each role does
System Administrator
is the super user of Vetri OS. The work done by the System Administrator is:
1.	Has overall access to, and an overall view of, every module in Vetri OS
2.	Creates login accounts and passwords for employees, assigns one or more roles to them, and activates or deactivates accounts
3.	Reviews and approves the login requests that HR raises for new employees and interns
4.	Creates and edits roles, and decides which permissions each role has
5.	Grants and revokes document access (Manage Access in Document Generator -> Library menu) and decides how long documents are kept
6.	Approves or rejects access requests from any user, and approves promotion requests raised by HR
7.	Creates and manages designations, departments and users
8.	Views the audit logs to see who did what across the system
9.	Manages document templates and generates letters in the Document Generator
10.	Uses the Email module to send emails to students, interns and employees
HR Administrator
runs the people side of Vetri OS. The work done by the HR Administrator is:
1.	Creates and edits employee records, including profile photo, department, designation, branch and employment type
2.	Monitors daily attendance for all employees and follows up on missing check-outs
3.	Reviews leave requests, approves or rejects them, and tracks leave balances
4.	Views the daily worklogs submitted by every employee
5.	Onboards new interns and external applicants through the onboarding checklist, and raises a login request to the System Administrator for them
6.	Drafts promotions, which the System Administrator then approves
7.	Maintains payroll references for each employee
8.	Handles employee exits through Exit Management
9.	Generates employee reports
10.	Generates offer letters and other documents, manages templates, and controls who can access a document
Employee
is a regular staff member who uses Vetri OS for their own daily work. The work done by an Employee is:
1.	Views their own dashboard after signing in
2.	Marks their own attendance and checks their attendance record in My Attendance
3.	Applies for leave and checks their leave balance in Apply Leave
4.	Submits their daily worklog in My Worklogs
5.	If they are a department lead, reviews their team's worklogs in Team Worklogs
6.	Opens documents they have access to in the Document Library and creates documents in Generate
7.	Requests a permission or document access from an admin through Request access, and tracks the answer
8.	Sees the projects they are assigned to, and receives notifications
Employee (Trainer)
is an Employee who teaches a training batch. The work done by a Trainer is:
1.	Does everything an Employee does (attendance, leave, worklogs, documents)
2.	Views the batches they teach in Training -> Batches
3.	Marks student attendance for their batch in Training -> Attendance
4.	Creates assignments and reviews student submissions in Assignments
5.	Views and generates training reports in Reports
6.	Conducts and records mock interviews in Mock Interview
7.	Tracks absent students and uploads class recordings in Absentees & Recordings
8.	Follows up on students who stop attending in Dropout Tracking
9.	Answers student questions (Ask Trainer) in Messages
Project Manager
leads projects and teams. The work done by a Project Manager is:
1.	Sees the Project Management menu for the projects they belong to: Project Dashboard, Project Team, Project Documents, Requirements, Kanban Board, Team Tasks, Intern Performance Review, Milestones, Deployments, Repository & Tech Stack, Team Messages and Change Requests
2.	Creates and edits project data (team, requirements, tasks, milestones, deployments, change requests); other team members can only view these pages
3.	Reviews intern performance and recommends internship completion or extension in Internship Completion/Extension
4.	Uses Client Management for their projects: Meetings / Call Log, Follow-Ups, Client Requests, Payments, Approval Documents and My Clients
5.	Views, creates and updates training batches and training data
6.	Views employee data and can update employee records, and views reports
7.	Opens documents in the Document Library
8.	Uses Email (Overview and Compose) and Request access
Business Team
handles the student and internship business side. The work done by the Business Team is:
1.	Views and manages all enrolled students in All Students
2.	Approves or rejects internship requests in Internship Approvals
3.	Approves internship completion and extension requests in Completion & Extension Approvals
4.	Assigns a reporting manager to interns in Assign Reporting Manager
5.	Handles new student enquiries in Enquiries
6.	Tracks fee payments and converts enquiries into enrolled students in Fee & Conversion
7.	Creates new training batches in New Batch
8.	Sends welcome emails to new students in Welcome Emails
9.	Views the Audit Logs to see who did what across the system
10.	Uses the Email module, including Bulk email to students and interns
11.	Opens, creates and updates documents in the Document Library and Generate
Intern
is a trainee working inside the company on a real project. The work done by an Intern is:
1.	Views their internship details (duration, reporting manager, status) in My Internship
2.	Marks and checks their own attendance in My Attendance
3.	Views and updates the tasks given by their project lead in My Tasks
4.	Raises and tracks bug or testing reports in Testing Reports
5.	Submits their daily work report in Daily Work Report
6.	Applies for leave in Apply Leave
7.	Asks their project lead questions through Ask Project Lead
8.	Views their performance review in My Performance
9.	Views the project they are assigned to in My Project
10.	Opens documents shared with them in the Document Library
11.	Uses Email (Overview and Compose) and Request access
Student
is an enrolled learner who uses Vetri OS only for their own course. The work done by a Student is:
1.	Views their own dashboard after signing in (My Dashboard)
2.	Checks their class attendance in My Attendance
3.	Asks their trainer questions through Ask Trainer
4.	Takes tests and views results in Assessments
5.	Tracks their course progress in Progress
6.	Views and submits the assignments given by the trainer in Assignments
7.	Watches recordings of classes they missed in Recordings
8.	Views their own performance reports in Reports
9.	Uses Email (Overview and Compose) and Request access
10.	Has no administrative permissions and cannot see other students' data
